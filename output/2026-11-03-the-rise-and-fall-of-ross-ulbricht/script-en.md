## 7. Two Life Sentences and the Free Ross Movement

*[photo: Katherine B. Forrest]*

In May 2015, Judge Katherine B. Forrest delivers a staggering verdict in a Manhattan courtroom. Ross Ulbricht is sentenced to double life imprisonment plus forty years without the possibility of parole. He is also ordered to forfeit an astronomical one hundred eighty-three million dollars.

*[photo: FREE ROSS]*

Outside the courtroom walls, a relentless mother refuses to let her son fade into the federal prison system. Lyn Ulbricht launches the Free Ross campaign, mobilizing a coalition of libertarian politicians, prison reformers, and early cryptocurrency adopters. What begins as a localized protest quickly transforms into a global movement demanding judicial reform.
